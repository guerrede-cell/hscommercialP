about

HS Commercial Projects is a family-owned commercial cleaning company based in the Bronx, proudly serving businesses throughout New York City. We provide dependable, high-quality cleaning services tailored to meet the unique needs of each client—from offices and retail stores to commercial facilities of all sizes.With a strong focus on consistency, professionalism, and attention to detail, our experienced team works hard to deliver a clean, safe, and welcoming environment for your staff and customers. We know that finding a reliable cleaning partner can be challenging, which is why we prioritize clear communication, flexible scheduling, and exceptional results every time.Led by owner Hugo, HS Commercial Projects is deeply committed to supporting the local business community. Hugo takes great pride in building lasting client relationships based on trust, quality, and care. When he's not managing operations, he enjoys spending time with his family and giving back to the Bronx community.
