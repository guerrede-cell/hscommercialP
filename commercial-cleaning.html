commercial Cleaning FAQ

What is included in commercial cleaning services?

Commercial cleaning services typically include dusting, vacuuming, trash removal, sanitizing surfaces, cleaning restrooms, and maintaining floors. We can also provide specialized services like window cleaning- *Interior Windows and Front Door Window Only*, carpet cleaning, and deep cleaning depending on your needs.

How often should my business be cleaned?

The ideal cleaning frequency for your business depends on factors like foot traffic, industry regulations, and the type of space. High-traffic businesses such as restaurants, retail stores, and healthcare facilities benefit from daily cleaning, while offices and businesses with moderate traffic may only need weekly service. For low-traffic spaces, bi-weekly or monthly cleaning may be sufficient. Regular professional cleaning not only keeps your business looking its best but also ensures a safe and welcoming environment for employees and customers. Need a custom cleaning schedule? We can help!
